This page is for notes and materials from my mini-course on algebraic functions and Riemann surfaces (after Klein).

A lot of this course has come out of conversations with Josh Batson, and his wonderful set of notes: [How to See a Meromorphic 1-Form](/files/Batson%20-%20How%20to%20See%20a%20Meromorphic%201-Form.pdf). This is a modern update of the first section of Felix Klein's [On Riemann's Theory of Algebraic Functions and their Integrals](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/36959/36959-pdf.pdf) (available thanks to Project Gutenberg). Klein's [Elementary Mathematics from an Advanced Viewpoint](https://ia801407.us.archive.org/5/items/elementarymathem032765mbp/elementarymathem032765mbp.pdf) is also worth reading.
