This page is for notes and materials for my mini-course on topological data analysis and persistent homology.

- [Lecture 1 – Topological Data Analysis](https://jpwolfson.files.wordpress.com/2016/06/lecture-1-topological-data-analysis1.pptx)

Good surveys of persistent homology and its uses in geometry and data analysis include:

- [Carlsson – Topology and Data (2009)](http://www.ams.org/journals/bull/2009-46-02/S0273-0979-09-01249-X/)
- [Edelsbrunner, Harer – Survey of Persistent Homology (2008)](https://users.cs.duke.edu/~edels/Papers/2008-B-02-PersistentHomology.pdf)
- [Weinberger – What is persistent homology? (2011)](http://www.ams.org/notices/201101/rtx110100036p.pdf)
- [Otter, Porter, Tillmann, Grindrod, and Harrington – A Roadmap for the Computation of Persistent Homology (2016)](http://arxiv.org/pdf/1506.08903.pdf)

A recent article on the use of persistent homology in studying flu strains is:

- [Chen, Carlsson, Rabadan – Topology of Viral Evolution (2013)](http://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC3831954/pdf/pnas.201313480.pdf)

Computer packages for implementing persistent homology computations include:

- [Javaplex](http://appliedtopology.github.io/javaplex/) (requires MatLab or other Java based system)
- [Perseus](http://www.sas.upenn.edu/~vnanda/perseus/) (requires C++ compiler)

[Applied Topology](http://appliedtopology.org/) is an online repository for conferences, software and article releases, and openings.
